BitRouter's core router is Apache 2.0 and can run inside infrastructure you control. BitRouter Cloud is the managed path. We do not package a separate enterprise suite today; teams with requirements beyond those two paths can work with us as design partners.

## Choose a deployment path

| | Self-hosted OSS | BitRouter Cloud | Enterprise design partnership |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Teams that want to own the request path, credentials, and routing policy | Teams that want a managed endpoint and provider network | Teams with specific security, procurement, or operating requirements |
| Operations | You run and upgrade BitRouter | Managed by BitRouter | Defined with your team |
| Provider access | Bring your own keys or local models | BitRouter-hosted models and optional BYOK | Depends on the agreed deployment |
| Commercial model | Apache 2.0; no BitRouter inference fee for self-hosted BYOK traffic | Usage-based pricing | Contact us |

## What ships today

The self-hosted router, configuration, routing policy, protocol adapters, and observability surface are open source. BitRouter Cloud adds a managed endpoint, hosted model access, account billing, and cloud request activity.

Requirements such as SSO, delegated RBAC, compliance certifications, enterprise audit workflows, and contractual SLAs are not a generally available product tier today. If one of those is a blocker, [tell us what you need](https://cal.com/bitrouter/founder-call) before planning a migration.
