## Service Description

BitRouter is an AI proxy service that routes requests to various AI providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others. We provide a unified interface for accessing multiple AI models with flexible payment options.

## Payment Terms

OSS self-hosting has no BitRouter platform or request fee; you remain responsible for infrastructure and provider charges. Cloud Auto Routing and Elastic Inference share early access pricing of $0.01 per million processed tokens. Processed usage is the verifiable actual input + output tokens processed by BitRouter Cloud, accumulated across requests. 0% markup on inference.

With BYOK, your provider bills model inference directly. Elastic Inference is charged at the supplier’s actual settled inference charge plus the Cloud platform fee. The platform fee is counted once per request, including when Auto Routing selects Elastic Inference; no percentage-based transaction fee applies. The purchase price cap includes supplier inference and the platform fee, with budget checks using input tokens and the full output limit. Enterprise services and pricing are governed by custom agreed terms.

Cloud platform usage and provider or supplier inference charges are recorded separately. Third-party payment-processing fees may apply when adding funds; they are separate from Cloud platform usage and inference charges.

We offer two payment methods:

- **Credits**: Pre-paid balance for API usage
- **x402 Crypto Payments**: Pay-per-request using cryptocurrency overpayments

All payments are final. Unused credits may be refunded upon request. Crypto overpayments are automatically credited to your account balance.
