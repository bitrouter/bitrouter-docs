OpenRouter built this category. One key, a very broad hosted catalog — if you've ever swapped providers without rewriting a line of code, that's largely their doing. We've learned plenty from them, and for a lot of teams they're still the right answer.

We're not trying to out-catalog them. BitRouter is a bet that the interesting problem has moved: not *how many models can you reach*, but *which one should this particular call go to* — and who owns the layer making that call. That's harder to build and slower to prove. It's the thing we want to build.

## Where we're genuinely different

**You can run the whole thing yourself.** BitRouter is Apache 2.0, top to bottom — `bro start` and the router lives in your VPC, your CI, or your laptop. OpenRouter is a hosted service. Self-hosting BitRouter keeps the routing policy, provider credentials, and request path in infrastructure you control.

**Self-hosted BYOK has no BitRouter inference fee.** Your provider bills you directly and BitRouter does not meter the request for payment. OpenRouter's hosted platform and BYOK allowances vary by plan; check its [current pricing](https://openrouter.ai/pricing) instead of treating a fee quoted here as permanent. BitRouter Cloud is a separate managed option with prices shown in the [live model catalog](/docs/overview/supported-models).

**The router learns your workload.** Every gateway can pick a model. BitRouter runs an [act → observe → evaluate → learn](/docs/overview/what-is-bitrouter) cycle over your actual runs, so routing tightens with every request instead of following a preset someone else tuned. That's the whole product — not a feature bolted onto a catalog.

## When OpenRouter is the better call

**You want the broadest hosted catalog.** OpenRouter's catalog is broader than BitRouter Cloud's deliberately curated agentic and coding set. With BitRouter you can still [bring your own model](/docs/customization/models#custom-endpoints-and-models), local or remote, but that is different from having every model available on one hosted account.

**You want free models.** OpenRouter surfaces free options for some models; BitRouter Cloud does not currently offer a free-model tier. If free hosted inference is a requirement, that is a concrete reason to choose OpenRouter.

**You need deeper enterprise tooling.** OpenRouter has more of it built out today. If there's a requirement we don't meet, see [team pricing](/pricing#teams) and tell us about the gap — we'd rather hear it than guess at it.

## Migrate from OpenRouter

For BitRouter Cloud, replace the OpenRouter base URL and credential:

```diff
- base_url="https://openrouter.ai/api/v1"
- api_key=OPENROUTER_API_KEY
+ base_url="https://api.bitrouter.ai/v1"
+ api_key=BITROUTER_API_KEY
```

Keep explicit model ids such as `openai/gpt-4o`; replace `openrouter/auto` with `bitrouter/auto`. You can remove OpenRouter-specific `HTTP-Referer`, `X-Title`, `transforms`, and `route` fields because BitRouter does not use them.

For a self-hosted deployment, [install and start BitRouter](/docs/overview/quickstart#run-self-hosted), provide your upstream provider keys, and point the client at `http://127.0.0.1:4356/v1`. Verify the models you need in the [catalog](/docs/overview/supported-models) before moving production traffic.
